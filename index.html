How to play

Tap or click a card to select it, then tap a highlighted target: an enemy for attacks, or the Khan for defense and abilities. Each card costs stamina (the number in its corner). Press End Turn when you are done.

Watch the icon above each enemy: a sword means it will attack you (for the number shown), a shield means it will defend, and a question mark means it is planning something unpleasant.

After every stage, choose a new card for your deck or an innate ability that works on its own. Can you defeat the King of the West?

Effects

D · Defend: blocks that much attack damage until your next turn.
E · Enrage: deal 50% more attack damage for that many turns.
W · Weaken: deal 25% less damage and gain 50% less defense for that many turns.

Hover or tap D, E, W and the enemy intent icons for a reminder.
